Introduction

Welcome to 10xDaily, a learning and knowledge application that provides daily vocabulary, daily news, quizzes, brain games, and tracks learning progress and streaks. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our application features. We collect the following personal and activity information:

  • Account Information: Name, Email address, Phone number, Date of birth, Password, and Avatar images.
  • Learning & Activity: Vocabulary learned, Quiz results, Quiz accuracy, Game scores and results, Learning progress, Streak information, XP (Experience Points), and Saved content/bookmarks.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising IDs, or financial/health information.

3

Automatically Collected / Technical Information

We do not automatically collect analytics or tracking data. Our application does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs like Google Analytics or Firebase.

4

How We Use Information

Your information is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage user accounts
  • To authenticate users
  • To provide application functionality
  • To save learning progress and user content/preferences
  • To track quiz and game results
  • To maintain learning streaks and XP
  • To provide user support, maintain security, fix bugs, and improve the application

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, personalization, or marketing.

5

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the third-party services required to operate the application. Our current service providers are:

  • Render: For application, backend hosting, and PostgreSQL database hosting.
  • Resend: For email delivery (such as support and feedback emails).

These service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services to us.

6

Security

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to backend and database services is strictly restricted.
  • User passwords are securely hashed before being stored in our database.
7

Data Retention

We retain your account and learning data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete the personal information associated with that account, except for information that must legally or legitimately be retained.

8

Account and Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data at any time through our in-app deletion process:

Profile → Personal Information → Delete Account

Alternatively, you can delete your account via our web-based account deletion page at:

10xdaily.online/delete-account

Completing this process will delete your associated personal data from our database in accordance with our deletion implementation.

9

Children

10xDaily is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information where prohibited by applicable law.

10

Cookies and Local Storage

Our application uses localStorage securely on your device solely for authentication (to store your access token), session management, and storing basic application preferences (such as theme preferences). We do not use tracking cookies.

11

User Privacy Rights

As a user, you have the right to:

  • Access and update your account information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Request information about your personal data.
  • Contact us regarding any privacy questions.
12

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material updates, we will change the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
